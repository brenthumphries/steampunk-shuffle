Keep the Bridge on your home screen

Steampunk Shuffle opens from your home screen like any other app, and keeps working without a connection once it has loaded.

On Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app, below or in Chrome's menu (⋮).

On iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the box with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The icon on your home screen is called Wheatstone.
