## Decisions made

- **Moonrise's "final round" is round 3, or round 2 when a player already holds a round win.** Chosen because a 2-0 sweep would otherwise leave the card dead in about half of matches. Rejected: round 3 only. Written into `design.md` §5.15. Brent to confirm.
- **`createMatch` takes `initialLocation: Card`, not the plan's `initialLocationId`.** The engine has no card registry by design; `resolveHouseLocation()` does the id lookup.
- **The Witching Hour reads "return the two lowest-point face-up cards on the table."** A Location has no controller, so "each player returns their own lowest" isn't expressible (known gap #3, `src/cards/data/README.md`). Rejected: a per-player `Target` extension, as too invasive for Build 1.
- **Bracket simulations cap the dial at `seasoned`.** Generating a Reichenbach Open bracket took 8 to 18 seconds on a laptop (legend-dial AI vs AI on a frozen Enter tap); the Wake would have been the same at 3 wins. Now about 1 second for both. This changes Reichenbach too. The player's own matches keep the real dial. `simulatedDifficulty()` in `bracket.ts`.
- **Spring-Heeled Jack is `tournamentOnly` with no first-win reward.** His card is the prize; a reward as well would pay it twice. Without the flag his `legend` tier would also have put him in the Reichenbach Open and Birthday Invitational fields.
- **The deck builder now shows owned extras.** That includes the four Seasoned reward cards, which had no tile before (the 4.0d note flagged it as not done). It was the only way to make owned event cards playable.
- **Balance edits stayed deck-local:** Thomas Marvel and Mrs Hall (Griffin), Renfield (Count, reused in Jack), Church Fete Stall reused for the Pharaoh. No shared card changed.
