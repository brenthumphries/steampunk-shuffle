## Also this session: draw mechanic change

### What I worked on

Changed the round's draw mechanic on Brent's direct instruction: a 4-card
opening hand, then a draw of 1 at the start of every turn in every round,
replacing the old 5-card hand plus a round-start batch draw of 3 (rounds 2
and 3 only). Reran the balance simulator against the new cadence and logged
the result.

### What got done

- **Engine (`src/engine/matchEngine.ts`).** Added named constants
  `OPENING_HAND_SIZE = 4` and `TURN_DRAW_AMOUNT = 1`. `createMatch` deals 4
  instead of 5. `startOfRound` no longer draws at all (the Transform step
  stays). `playTurn` draws 1 before checking whether the hand is empty.
- **`docs/design.md` §6.1, §6.2, and §13.2 rewritten** to match: draw 4 at
  setup, one draw at the start of every turn, the round-start draw sentence
  removed, and the tutorial's forced-draw description rewritten for the new
  per-turn cadence.
- **Tutorial deck reordered (`src/tutorial/tutorialScript.ts`).**
  `TUTORIAL_PLAYER_DECK` reordered, same 12 cards, no new content, so
  Police Whistle's own On Play draw still lands on Bramwell specifically
  instead of an earlier ordinary turn-draw, keeping the beer mat's "the
  whistle fetched you a card" line true. Traced the exact draw-event
  sequence against all 18 scripted turns to confirm it. `TUTORIAL_HOUSE_DECK`
  needed no change.
- **In-game House Rules copy (`src/ui/houseRulesScreen.ts`)** updated:
  "shuffle and draw 4", the per-turn draw sentence, and dropped the now-false
  "Hands don't otherwise refill mid-round" line.
- **Tests.** Two unit fixtures updated (`tests/unit/engine/
  tutorialRound1.test.ts`'s inline deck, one deck-count assertion in
  `tests/unit/engine/keywords.test.ts`); the other 39 unit test files needed
  no change. All 386 unit tests pass, `npm run typecheck` clean.
- **`npm run sim` re-run** (134.0s) and `docs/balance.md` rewritten with the
  new cadence's numbers.
- **CI caught an e2e test the unit sweep missed.** The first push (`bff7611`)
  broke all four browser E2E jobs: `tests/e2e/houseRules.spec.ts` still
  asserted the old "draws 3 more cards at the start of rounds 2 and 3"
  copy. Fixed in `e1f3631`. Full local Playwright run (134 passed, 1
  skipped, 0 failed) and CI run `35177368408` both green; Pages redeployed.
- **New `CLAUDE.md` gotcha** logging the balance shift: Starter-vs-Regular-
  tier win rates (design.md §12.2, target ~60%) moved from a 20-60% spread
  to a tighter 40-53% band, now under target across the board; AI-vs-random
  win rates rose; same-difficulty AI mirrors compressed toward a coin flip.
  Also flags that the pre-existing "wide variance" gotcha right below it
  already cited stale `docs/balance.md` numbers, independent of this
  session (a Nell Ashby re-pointing landed in between).
- **Commits, both pushed to `main` on Brent's instruction:** `bff7611` (the
  mechanic change, balance rerun, and gotcha) and `e1f3631` (the e2e fix).

### Decisions made

- **The per-turn draw applies to every round, not just round 1** (Brent,
  asked directly mid-session). The alternative considered: draws only
  during round 1, nothing at all in rounds 2 or 3, a sealed-hand back half.
  Brent picked "every round"; the old round-start batch draw is fully
  replaced, not layered underneath it.
- **No new tutorial content, only a reorder.** Worked out the exact
  draw-event sequence by hand against all 18 scripted turns and confirmed
  the existing 12-card player deck and 11-card house deck already cover
  every "needed by turn N" deadline once reordered. No filler cards added.
- **Balance numbers reported, not tuned.** The Starter-vs-Regular band
  moving under the ~60% target is logged as a gotcha, not adjusted here:
  the draw-mechanic change was Brent's design call, and re-balancing card
  points is a separate decision.
