## Decisions made

- Native here means the `--mode native` bundle served at `/` on Chromium (the repo's Native project); no iOS runtime, so Capacitor Preferences hydration is covered only by the unit tests.
- Restore and ledger-flow checks ran on copies of the upgraded profile with no match in progress, because a resumed match cannot reach Save & data without leaving it.
