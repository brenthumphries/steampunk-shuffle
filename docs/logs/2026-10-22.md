## What I worked on

Brent supplied a 10-second Gemini film of the pub and asked for it to play when the game launches. Added it as a launch intro in the web and native builds. Done in a Cowork session; nothing committed, pushed or shipped.

## What got done

- **Launch intro screen.** `src/ui/introScreen.ts` plays `public/video/intro.mp4` (1280x720, H.264/AAC, 4.95 MB, already faststart) inline, with `public/video/intro-poster.webp` as the first frame. Tap anywhere skips. It plays on every launch, before the dedication and everything else; `main.ts` now boots through `bootGame()` after it.
- **It cannot trap the player.** A media error, a refused play, or no `playing` event within 8 s calls `onDone`. If the browser refuses sound, play() retries muted. It starts muted when the game's mute setting is on.
- **Skipped for automation and Reduce Motion.** `?intro=0` always skips and `?intro=1` always plays. Playwright's `navigator.webdriver` skips by default, so no existing spec waits on the film.
- **Offline.** `mp4` added to the Workbox precache globs in `vite.config.ts`. The file is 4,950,388 bytes against the 5 MB per-file cap, so a larger re-encode needs the cap raised. `public/` still passes the 25 MB budget test.
- **Tests.** `tests/unit/ui/introScreen.test.ts` (new, 12), a launch-intro case in `tests/unit/main.test.ts` (which now pins `?intro=0` elsewhere), `tests/e2e/intro.spec.ts` (new). Run in a cloud clone of 8f20437 plus these changes: `tsc` clean, 628 of 628 unit tests, intro and smoke specs on Chromium (Pages and Native). Not run: Safari, Firefox, Edge, real playback on an iPhone.

## Decisions made

- **Letterboxed, not cropped.** The film is 16:9 and the phone is portrait; `object-fit: cover` would cut the faces out, so it plays as a strip in the middle on the `--ground` colour. Rejected: cover. Open for Brent: a portrait re-render would fill the screen.
- **Plays every launch, tap to skip.** Brent asked for "on launching the game". Claude's reading; a first-launch-only rule is a one-line change.
- **Skipped under Reduce Motion**, following the project's reduced-motion convention. Claude's call.
