## What I worked on

January step J-A (real moon phases and Full Moon +N), started early on Brent's say-so. Branch `claude/aether-voyage-j-a`. No event pack, visitors or event cards: those are J-C.

## What got done

- **`src/sky/moon.ts`.** Meeus ch. 49 new and full moon instants (periodic terms, 14 planetary arguments, ΔT 2005-2050). Exports `moonPhase(date)` (8 named states, illumination 0-1), `isFullMoonNight(date)` (day before, of, after, in local time), `moonEventInstant`. No clock inside.
- **Full Moon +N.** `Keywords.fullMoon`, validator, `effectivePoints` (face-up only, stacks with Friend and Moonrise), `MatchState.fullMoon` set once by `createMatch({ startDate })`, AI nudge in `cardPotentialValue`, chip "Full Moon +N", House Rules entry, design.md §5.17. `matchScreen` passes `today()`; simulations and bracket sims pass nothing, so no moon.
- **Old saved matches.** `fullMoon` is optional; a saved match without it loads and reads as false (test in `activeMatch.test.ts`). No save-version change, no new localStorage key.
- **Hub moon.** `src/ui/hubMoon.ts` draws eight SVG states, glow on full; tap opens a beer mat from `src/sky/moonLines.ts` (phase title, Sir Charles line, plus the Hampstead line on full-moon nights). `?now=&preview=1` reaches it through `today()`.
- **Tests.** `tests/unit/sky/moon.test.ts`, `tests/unit/ui/hubMoon.test.ts`, Full Moon cases in `seasonalKeywords.test.ts`, property test now runs half its games on a full-moon night, `tests/e2e/moon.spec.ts` (full, new, waxing gibbous, dev clock).
