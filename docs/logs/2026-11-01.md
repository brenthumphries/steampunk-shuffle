## What I worked on

Monthly content plan step N-B: the sixth family, the Coven, and the Scry effect (engine, AI, overlay, docs). No Coven cards (N-D authors them). Built in a cloud clone on branch `claude/cunning-folk-n-b`.

## What got done

- **Schema.** `"coven"` is in `FAMILIES`; `{ effect: "scry"; amount }` is in the `Effect` union. `validateCard` accepts Scry on any card with a controller and rejects it on a Location (`src/cards/cardValidator.ts`). The Heather frame variant and `--family-coven` from N-A are now live, plus deck-tile and back-room tile colours, the deck builder's family label, the art-prompt palette line and the sim's Coven curve target (2.6).
- **Engine.** `scryDeck` in `src/engine/matchEngine.ts`; `PlayOptions.chooseScry` (`ScryChooser`, `ScryChoice` in `matchTypes.ts`) mirrors `chooseTargets`. No chooser means the order is left alone, as does an answer that is not a permutation of the cards shown. An empty look never asks.
- **AI.** `aiScryChooser` in `src/ai/aiOpponent.ts`: a card stays on top when its `cardPotentialValue` (which carries the Persist, Friend and Moonrise nudges) is at least the average of the AI's own deck, best first; the rest go to the bottom. `playAITurn`, `tools/sim.ts` and `tools/curve.ts` pass it for the played move only. Inside the search a Scry is a no-op, so the legend dial costs what it did. Scry cards get a +0.25 hand-potential nudge.
- **UI.** `src/ui/scryOverlay.ts` (two rows, tap to move a card between them, earlier/later arrows, Done), draft model in `src/match/scryDraft.ts`, and `src/match/scryPreview.ts`, which dry-runs the play to learn which cards a Scry will show before the turn commits. `matchScreen.ts` has a `scry` phase between Play and the commit. Scry chip on cards (`cardText.ts`) and a House Rules entry with the overview's reminder text.
- **Docs.** design.md: a Coven row in §4 (renamed "six families"), the Coven 2.6 curve and the two archetypes, new §5.16 Scry, a hexes note in §16.
- **Tests.** `tests/unit/engine/scry.test.ts` (N larger than the deck, empty deck, all-to-bottom, all-to-top, reorder, mix, bad answers, determinism, a 300-game multiset property test, validator cases), `tests/unit/match/scry.test.ts`, `tests/unit/ai/aiScry.test.ts` (all three dials play whole Scry matches), and `tests/e2e/scry.spec.ts` (seeds a resumed match holding a test-only Scry 3 card). `tests/unit/ui/almanackFrame.test.ts` now expects "coven" in `FAMILIES`.

## Decisions made

- **Human Scry is collected by dry run, not by a new engine phase.** playTurn is synchronous, and the cards a Scry sees depend on the turn-start draw. The UI replays the play with a chooser that throws on the first unanswered Scry, asks about those cards, and repeats. The engine is deterministic, so the committed play sees what was shown. Rejected: making the engine pausable.
- **No dev-fixture URL for the Playwright test.** The existing pattern of seeding an `active-match` save (as `unflipTarget.spec.ts` does) holds a test-only card with no new production code path.
- **The player cannot back out of a Scry** once Play is confirmed; Done commits the turn. Cancel is available before that (staging).
